## KB-DEV-01: Which browsers and devices are supported
The platform supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop. On phones and tablets, use the official iOS app (iOS 15 or later) or Android app (Android 9 or later). Internet Explorer is not supported. If a page looks broken or buttons do nothing, update your browser first, because outdated versions are the most common cause of display problems.

## KB-DEV-02: Clearing the browser cache and cookies to fix display problems
Many lesson page problems (blank pages, buttons that do nothing, stale content) are fixed by clearing the cache. In Chrome or Edge press Ctrl+Shift+Delete, choose "Cached images and files" and "Cookies", and clear them for the last 24 hours, then reload the platform and sign in again. In Safari use Develop > Empty Caches. As a quick test, open the lesson in a private window: if it works there, a browser extension or cached file is the cause.

## KB-DEV-03: Ad blockers and extensions that break lessons
Some ad blockers, privacy extensions and VPNs block the video player, the quiz scripts or the coding sandbox, which shows up as endless loading or a missing play button. Turn off extensions for the platform domain, or test in a private window with extensions disabled. If you are on a company or school network, a firewall can block the same resources and your IT team may need to allow the platform domain.
