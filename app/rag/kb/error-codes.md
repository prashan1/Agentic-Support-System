## KB-ERR-01: Error 403 Forbidden on a lesson page or video
Error 403 means the server understood the request but will not allow it. Common causes: your session expired (sign out and sign in again), you are not enrolled in this course, or a VPN or proxy is changing your location or IP address. Try signing in again, disable the VPN, and reload the lesson. If the course is listed in My Courses and the 403 continues after signing in again, contact support with the lesson link and the time of the error.

## KB-ERR-02: Error 404 Page not found
Error 404 means the page does not exist at that address. This usually happens with an old bookmark or a mistyped link, or when a lecture was moved during a course update. Go to My Courses and open the lesson from the course outline instead of using the saved link. If a lesson listed in the outline gives 404, report the lesson title to support.

## KB-ERR-03: Error 429 Too many requests
Error 429 means too many requests were sent from your connection in a short time, for example from refreshing repeatedly or from a script. Wait 5 minutes and try again. If you share a network with many users, the limit can be reached sooner. Do not keep refreshing, as this extends the wait.

## KB-ERR-04: Error 500 or 503 Something went wrong on our side
Errors 500 and 503 are problems on the platform's servers, not on your device. Wait a few minutes and reload. Check the status page for known incidents. If the error continues for more than 30 minutes and the status page shows no incident, contact support with a screenshot and the time it happened.

## KB-ERR-05: Error E-1042 Quiz submission failed
E-1042 appears when a quiz answer could not be saved, usually because the connection dropped while pressing Submit Quiz or the app is out of date. Your answers are kept for 10 minutes. Reconnect, reopen the quiz and press Submit again. If it fails again, update the app to the latest version, clear the app cache, and retry. Do not restart the quiz attempt, as that can use up an attempt.
