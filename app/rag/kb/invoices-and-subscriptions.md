## KB-INV-01: Download an invoice or receipt
Invoices are generated for every successful payment. Open Account, then Billing, then Payment history, and choose Download invoice next to the purchase. The invoice is a PDF and is available a few minutes after payment.

## KB-INV-02: Add a GST number or change billing details on an invoice
Billing name, address and GST number must be entered before payment because an issued invoice cannot be edited. If the details are wrong, support can cancel the invoice and issue a corrected one within 30 days of purchase, using the transaction ID.

## KB-SUB-01: Cancel a subscription
Cancel from Account, then Subscription, then Cancel plan. You keep access until the end of the current billing period and are not charged again. Cancelling does not delete the account. Cancellation does not automatically refund the current period.

## KB-SUB-02: Subscription renewal and billing date
Monthly subscriptions renew automatically on the same date each month, and annual subscriptions renew once a year. A reminder email is sent 3 days before renewal. The renewal is charged to the card or UPI mandate saved at purchase.

## KB-SUB-03: Subscription refund rule
A subscription charge can be refunded within 48 hours of an automatic renewal if the plan was not used since the renewal. The first purchase of a subscription follows the standard 7 day refund window. Refund eligibility is confirmed by the billing team.

## KB-SUB-04: Renewal failed or plan was downgraded
If a renewal payment fails, the plan stays active for a 3 day grace period and the payment is retried. After the grace period the account is moved to the free plan. Update the payment method in Billing, then choose Retry payment to restore the plan.
