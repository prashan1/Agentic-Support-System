## KB-APP-01: The mobile app crashes or closes when I press Submit Quiz
If the app closes when you tap Submit Quiz, update to the latest app version first, because older versions had a known crash on quiz submission. Then force-close the app, clear its cache (Android: Settings > Apps > Cache; iPhone: offload and reinstall the app), free up at least 500 MB of storage, and try again on Wi-Fi. Your quiz answers are kept for 10 minutes. If the crash continues after updating, send a bug report from Profile > Help > Report a problem so the team can see the crash log.

## KB-APP-02: Downloading lessons for offline viewing
In the mobile app, tap the download icon next to a lecture to save it for offline viewing. Downloads need free storage and stay available for 30 days without an internet connection check. If a download stops at a percentage, switch to Wi-Fi, keep the app open, and retry. Offline downloads are only available in the mobile apps, not in the web browser.

## KB-APP-03: Push notifications are not arriving
If you do not receive reminders or announcements, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone settings and that battery saver is not restricting background activity. Inside the app, open Profile > Notifications and make sure the categories you want are on. Signing out and in again re-registers your device for notifications.
