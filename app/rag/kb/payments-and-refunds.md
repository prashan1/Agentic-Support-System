## KB-PAY-01: Payment failed or was declined
A payment is declined when the bank rejects it, the card has expired, or the billing details do not match the card. Check the card number, expiry date and billing address, then try again or use another card or UPI. Declines are decided by the bank, so if the details are correct, contact your bank. No money is kept when a payment fails; any temporary hold is released by the bank within 5 to 7 business days.

## KB-PAY-02: Money was deducted but the course is not unlocked
After a successful payment, access is normally unlocked within 15 minutes. If money was deducted and the course is still locked after 30 minutes, share the transaction ID so support can verify the payment. Do not pay a second time. Verified payments are activated manually, usually within one business day.

## KB-PAY-03: Charged twice for the same purchase (duplicate charge)
Two charges for one purchase can happen if the payment page was refreshed or submitted twice. Only one enrollment is created. The duplicate amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the duplicate is confirmed. Share both transaction IDs so the duplicate can be verified.

## KB-PAY-04: Accepted payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI and net banking. Wallets and cash payments are not supported. Prices are charged in INR, so international cards may include a currency conversion fee set by the card issuer.

## KB-REF-01: Refund eligibility window
A course is eligible for a full refund within 7 days of purchase if less than 20 percent of the content has been watched. After 7 days, or after more than 20 percent has been watched, the purchase is not refundable. Subscriptions follow the separate subscription refund rule.

## KB-REF-02: How to request a refund and how long it takes
Request a refund from the Billing page of your account by choosing the purchase and selecting Request refund, or share the transaction ID with support. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds cannot be paid to a different card or account. An agent cannot promise a refund; eligibility is confirmed by the billing team.

## KB-REF-03: Refund not received yet
If a refund was approved more than 7 business days ago and has not arrived, check your bank statement for a refund entry, since some banks label it with a different merchant name. If it is still missing after 10 business days, share the transaction ID with support so the refund reference number can be traced.
