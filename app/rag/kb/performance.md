## KB-PERF-01: Pages load slowly or lessons take a long time to open
If pages are slow to load, test your connection speed first and try another network. Close unused tabs, clear the browser cache, and disable extensions. A slow page is different from a buffering video: if only the video stutters, use the quality settings in the player instead. If many users in your region report slowness, check the status page for a known incident.

## KB-PERF-02: Recommended internet speed and system requirements
For a smooth experience we recommend at least 5 Mbps download speed for HD video, 4 GB of RAM, and a browser updated in the last 3 months. The coding sandbox works best with a desktop or laptop. Phones and tablets are fully supported for video lessons and quizzes, but not recommended for long coding exercises.
