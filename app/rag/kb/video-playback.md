## KB-VID-01: Video keeps buffering or the spinner never stops
If a lesson video keeps buffering or the loading spinner never stops, first check your connection: video needs at least 3 Mbps for 720p and 5 Mbps for 1080p. Lower the quality with the gear icon in the player (choose 480p or Auto). Pause the video for 30 seconds so it can load ahead, then resume. If it still spins, close other tabs or downloads that use bandwidth, refresh the lesson page, and try another browser. If one specific lecture always freezes at the same time mark, note the lecture title and timestamp and contact support, because the file itself may be damaged.

## KB-VID-02: Video plays but there is no sound
If a video plays without sound, check that the player is not muted (speaker icon) and that the browser tab is not muted (right-click the tab). Then check your device volume and the selected audio output, especially if Bluetooth headphones are connected. Refreshing the page fixes most cases. If only one lecture has no audio while others work, report the lecture title to support so the audio track can be re-uploaded.

## KB-VID-03: Subtitles or captions are missing
Captions are available on most lectures. Click the CC button in the player to turn them on and choose a language. If the CC button is missing, captions have not been published for that lecture yet. New lectures can take up to 48 hours after release to receive captions. You can request captions for a specific lecture through the Contact Support page.

## KB-VID-04: Video does not resume where I stopped
Playback position is saved automatically every 15 seconds while you are signed in. If a video restarts from the beginning, make sure you were signed in on the same account, that your browser allows cookies for the platform, and that you are not using a private or incognito window, which does not keep progress. Progress can take a minute to sync between your phone and your computer.
